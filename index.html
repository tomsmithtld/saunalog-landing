Protocols

Research-backed programs

Huberman Protocol

57 min/week, 80-100C

2-3 sessions per week, 19-28 min each. The most cited target in sauna research.

Soeberg Principle

57 min/week + cold finish

Same heat exposure, but end each session with brief cold. Maximum metabolic benefit.

Beginner

30 min/week, 70-80C

2 sessions of 15 min. Lower temperature. For people building their heat tolerance.

The 57 minute number

Regular sauna use is linked to reduced cardiovascular mortality, improved recovery, and better sleep quality. The research keeps piling up.

57 minutes per week across 2-3 sessions is the threshold that keeps showing up in the literature. That's what Huberman recommends, and it's the target SaunaLog is built around.

The app doesn't tell you what to do. It just tracks whether you're hitting that number, and shows you how consistent you've been.

Sources: Laukkanen et al. (JAMA Internal Medicine, 2015), Huberman Lab Podcast, Dr. Susanna Soeberg research
